Overnight Fire HMM

A ranger station gets hourly readings for 12 hours. By the end a fire is clearly burning. Two hidden variables (fuel and fire) evolve over time, and two sensors watch them. Compare what an operator believed in real time (forward), what an investigator believes with every reading in hand (smoothed), and the single most likely history (Viterbi).

Why it is still an HMM

Merge the two hidden variables into one joint state with 2 × 2 = 4 values. Forward, smoothing and Viterbi then run unchanged on that state. Because each sensor depends on only one variable, the emission probability is just the product of the two sensor probabilities.

  • Forward uses readings up to hour t.
  • Smoothed uses all 12 hours, so later readings revise earlier hours.
  • Viterbi picks the most likely complete history, which can differ from the hour-by-hour most likely values.
  • Adding a third binary hidden variable would double the joint state to 8. It grows exponentially.
